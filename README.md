# Safebound

build a healthtech website for a startup in early phase. the name of the startup is SafeBound and is working with behavioural healthcare, specifically addiction and eating disorders to use biomarkers to provide support during discharge. the copy for the website is:
Hero: Building a person-controlled continuity layer between behavioural-health care and everyday life





Body section: When someone leaves intensive treatment for an eating disorder or another behavioural-health condition, there is usually a sharp drop from structured care into messy, unpredictable reality.



In that period, people can feel very alone with urges, worries, and coping plans that are hard to use when life is happening in real time. Care teams, meanwhile, only see short appointments and have to piece together what happened after the fact.



Other section: SafeBound is an early-stage project exploring whether a person-controlled digital companion could help fill that gap. We are starting with post-discharge eating-disorder recovery and looking at how people’s own patterns, context, and optional physiological signals might support gentle, just-in-time help between appointments, without trying to diagnose or replace clinical care.



Reach out to us section: If you have lived experience of leaving specialised eating-disorder care or addiction treatment and are willing to talk about what life between appointments is really like, your perspective would help us see what support feels genuinely helpful and what feels harmful or intrusive.



If you work in this world as a clinician, therapist, researcher, discharge coordinator, or on digital health and privacy, we’d also like to understand how post-discharge support actually works in your setting, and where a person-controlled layer like SafeBound might fit, or why it shouldn’t.





CTA: If this resonates with your experience, send a short email about who you are and how you see this problem. 



We’ll reply with a few possible times and a simple overview of the interview.
